# Greetings

A person can leave a short greeting for someone else, or leave it unaddressed.

## Language

**Greeting**:
One stored message, identified by the server.
_Avoid_: Note, post, message (when that means the whole record)

**Message**:
The text of a greeting. It is always present.
_Avoid_: Body, content, description

**Recipient**:
The name of the person the greeting is for. A greeting may have no recipient.
_Avoid_: User, owner, addressee
